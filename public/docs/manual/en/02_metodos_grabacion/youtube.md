# Transcribing a YouTube video

Paste the URL of a public YouTube video and Diktalo returns its full transcript, ready to summarise, analyse or query with **Ask Diktalo**.

---

## How to do it

1.  Press the **+** button in the sidebar header, or the **YouTube** card on the home screen.
2.  Paste the video URL.
3.  Choose the **transcript style** and the **output language**.
4.  Tick the rights confirmation box.
5.  Press **Transcribe**.

The result is saved as a regular recording: you can rename it, move it to a project, generate summaries with any template and ask the chat about it.

---

## Transcript style: Clean or Verbatim

This choice matters more than it looks, especially when translating.

*   **Clean** (default): removes filler words, hesitations and false starts, and fixes grammar. **Keeps everything that was said, in its original order** — it does not summarise or omit. It renders the target language naturally rather than word for word.
*   **Verbatim**: transcribes exactly as spoken, hesitations and repetitions included.

**Why Clean is the default for video:** a translated transcript cannot be verbatim. The moment you translate, it stops being a verbatim record, and asking for both produces text calqued from the original language, with idioms that mean nothing.

Clean transcripts carry a visible **"Edited transcript"** badge in the detail view. If you need a verbatim record with evidentiary value, use **Verbatim**.

---

## Output language

You can transcribe into a language other than the one spoken in the video. Spanish, English, Portuguese, French, German and Italian are available.

Useful for watching content in English and keeping the text in your own language directly, with no separate translation step.

---

## Templates built for video

When generating a summary you will find a **Video** filter in the left column, with the templates that work best on this kind of content. Among them:

*   **Step-by-step Tutorial:** turns a tutorial into a followable procedure, keeping commands and URLs exactly as spoken.
*   **Competitor Sheet:** for other companies' demos and webinars. Separates what they actually show from what they merely mention.
*   **Cited References and Data:** extracts people, companies, tools, figures and dates with their timestamps.
*   **Questions and Answers:** for interviews and podcasts.
*   **Social Media Script:** hooks, quotable excerpts, LinkedIn post and thread.

---

## What it consumes

The video consumes minutes from your plan like any other recording, based on its real duration.

Before processing, Diktalo checks the video length. If it does not fit in your remaining minutes, you are warned **without consuming anything**.

---

## Limits and what will not work

*   **Public videos only.** Private or unlisted ones cannot be read.
*   **Live broadcasts cannot be transcribed.** Wait until the broadcast ends.
*   **75 minutes maximum per video.** Longer ones exceed the model output limit and the transcript would arrive incomplete.
*   **YouTube only.** Vimeo, Loom and other platforms are not supported for now: those would require downloading the file, which goes against their terms of service.

---

## About the video and rights

Diktalo **never downloads or stores** the video. The URL is sent to Google, which processes its own platform, and Diktalo only keeps the resulting text.

On the recording page you will see the video embedded so you can consult it, next to a link to open it on YouTube.

A verbatim transcript of someone else's video is a reproduction of a copyrighted work. Before transcribing you must confirm you have the right to do so, and that responsibility is yours.
